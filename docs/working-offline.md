# Working offline

Tally keeps your groups on your device, so it opens with no connection and shows every group, its expenses, payments and balances as they were when
it last synced.

## What works with no connection

- **Recording an expense or a payment, changing or deleting one, and commenting.** What you record is on your device at once, and sent when you are
  back online, in the background. If someone else changed the same expense meanwhile, the change that reaches the server last stands whole, and the
  other is kept in the expense's history, where its author can restore it.
- **Reading everything you have:** groups, balances, a person's page, Insights.
- **The unread count** in the bell, as it was when you were last online.

If the server refuses something you recorded offline (the group was closed meanwhile, or you were removed from it), it is not lost: it waits under
**Couldn't sync**, with the reason, and you can send it again, move it to another group, or let it go.

## What needs a connection

- **Signing in,** and the first time you open Tally on a device, which brings your groups to it and unlocks what is written in them.
- **Making a group, adding or removing people, invitations, roles, closing, reopening and deleting a group.** Each is decided by the server at once;
  offline, the screen says it needs a connection and nothing changes.
- **Settings, notifications, reminders, Activity, an expense's history, downloading your data and importing a file.** Each says so offline.

## Where it stops

- **On an iPhone or iPad, Tally syncs only while it is open.** Safari has no background sync, so open Tally once you are back online to send what you
  recorded.
- **A browser can clear what a site keeps,** for instance when the device is short of space. Tally asks the browser to keep its data, but changes not
  yet sent could still be lost if it clears it. Opening Tally online sends them.
- **On a computer you share,** your groups stay in the browser until you sign out. Signing out deletes them from the device, after trying to send what
  is waiting.
