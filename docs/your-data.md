# Your data

All of it is in **Settings, Data.**

## Download your data

**Download your data** gives one file of your profile and every group you are in, whole: its members by name, its expenses with who paid and how it
was split, their comments, and its payments. No one else's email address is in it.

The file is made **in your browser**, from this device, with what was written opened, since only your devices can open it. So **the file is plain
text**: anyone who has it can read your groups' descriptions and comments. Keep it somewhere safe. If a group is not unlocked on this device yet, what
was written in it is left out, and the screen says how much.

On a Sovereign instance, a line on your activity page there says you downloaded it.

## Import a file

**Import a file** turns a Tally download into **new groups**, with you as their owner. Everyone else in them comes in as **not on Tally yet**, by name,
with everything they shared; nobody is added to anything without being asked, and you can invite them when you are ready. Importing the same file twice
makes the groups twice.

The file is checked whole before anything is made, and either every group is made or none is. A file from a newer version of Tally is refused, with a
sentence that says so.

## Delete your account

**Delete your account** removes everything of yours: your account, your email address, your keys and devices, what you were told and chose to be told.
Your **name stays** on the expenses and payments you shared, shown as "left Tally", so that the other members' balances still add up; once no group
shows it any more, it goes too. A group with no one else in it goes with you.

On a Sovereign instance, deleting your account there deletes it here as well.
